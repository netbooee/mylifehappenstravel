By Tony & Sharon · Traveled April 2026

We built this trip around three of Italy's most storied cities, with day trips worked in. Five nights in Florence gave us time to breathe: the Uffizi, the Duomo, a day trip to Rome, a morning in Pisa and an afternoon cycling around Lucca. Three nights in Venice added the islands of Murano and Burano, a full day in the Prosecco Hills, and evenings spent getting lost in the calli on purpose. A final night back in Florence made for an easy departure.

We paced it on purpose: tours and sights in the morning, loose afternoons, and dinner reservations to look forward to after a long day. We pre-booked almost every big museum ticket, train and tour, and we noted where the timing had to be exact and where we could relax.

The plan, day by day

Day 1 Fri, Apr 3 · Florence

Landing in Florence & a first aperitivo

We fly out of Newark on Thursday afternoon and land in Florence on Friday with plenty of daylight left. We drop our bags, freshen up and head out. Tonight is for easing in, because tomorrow's 8:30 AM walking tour is one of the biggest mornings of the trip.

  1. Overnight flight from Newark to Florence (leaving Thu, Apr 2)
  2. ~4:00 PMCheck in at Hotel L'Orologio Firenze on Piazza Santa Maria Novella, perfectly placed for exploring the center on foot Booked ahead
  3. Late afternoonWander Piazza Santa Maria Novella and the streets around it, then our first Italian aperitivo: a Negroni or Aperol Spritz at a café with outdoor seating. Mercato Centrale is close by if we'd rather graze
  4. 8:00 PMDinner at L'Osteria di Giovanni, a classic Florentine trattoria Booked ahead

Tip: Don't overdo the first night: the Uffizi tour starts at 8:30 AM sharp.

Day 2 Sat, Apr 4 · Florence

The Uffizi, the Duomo & the bell tower

Our biggest day in Florence. We do a small-group Renaissance tour in the morning, climb Giotto's Campanile on a timed ticket after lunch, then visit Santa Croce, walk the shopping streets and catch sunset over the city.

  1. 7:15 AMBreakfast at the hotel and out the door by 8
  2. 8:15 AMMeet at the Florence & Global Tour office (Via Guelfa 12R) for the 4-hour small-group Uffizi and Accademia tour with 8:30 AM timed entry and a Duomo walk (Viator) Booked ahead
  3. 12:30 PMCappuccino and a light lunch near the Arno, then the Ponte Vecchio, the only Florentine bridge not destroyed in WWII, and Piazza della Signoria, an open-air sculpture gallery with the Neptune Fountain and a replica of Michelangelo's David
  4. 2:00 PMTake in Santa Maria del Fiore (the Duomo) and the sheer scale of Brunelleschi's dome from Piazza del Duomo
  5. 2:15 PMClimb Giotto's Campanile for sweeping views over the whole city. We need to be in line by 2:05 and back down by 3:00 Booked ahead
  6. 3:00 PMRest, or grab a sandwich at Il Bufalo Trippone or Pino's Sandwiches / Salumeria Verdi
  7. 3:45 PMBasilica of Santa Croce with an audio tour. Michelangelo and Galileo are buried here Booked ahead
  8. 5:00 PMMuseo dell'Opera del Duomo (included with the bell tower ticket, open until 7 PM), if we still have the energy Booked ahead
  9. 6:00 PMGelato and the shopping streets: Via del Corso, Via dei Calzaiuoli, Via Roma to Piazza della Repubblica's carousel and cafés, and the luxury shops of Via de' Tornabuoni
  10. 7:00 PMSunset at Piazzale Michelangelo or the quieter San Miniato al Monte just above it. The sun sets around 7:45
  11. 8:30 PMDinner at Osteria Lucignolo, booked through TheFork with 30% off food Booked ahead

Tip: Timed entry at the Campanile is strict. Be in line by 2:05 PM and leave backpacks and large bags at the hotel.

Day 3 Sun, Apr 5 · Florence

Easter: the Explosion of the Cart, Pitti Palace & a pasta class

Easter Sunday in Florence. We start with the Scoppio del Carro outside the Duomo, which happens only once a year. Then we cross the river for the Bardini Gardens and Palazzo Pitti and spend the afternoon making pasta and gelato from scratch.

  1. 8:30 AMA slow, full breakfast at the hotel, since there's no proper meal until the cooking class
  2. 9:30 AMScoppio del Carro in Piazza del Duomo. In this tradition dating to the 14th century, a cart loaded with fireworks is lit by a dove-shaped rocket during Easter mass for good fortune. It starts around 10–11 AM; arrive early for the crowds
  3. 11:30 AMFree time to pick and choose. Palazzo Pitti can be entered before the timed tour slot
  4. 12:00 PMBardini Gardens: the baroque staircase, wisteria tunnel, panoramic terraces and fountains Booked ahead
  5. 1:00 PM30-minute guided tour of the Royal Apartments at Palazzo Pitti Booked ahead
  6. After PittiBoboli Gardens (free entry today) if the lines are reasonable, or the Oltrarno's artisan workshops and leather shops
  7. 2:25 PM20-minute walk to the cooking school
  8. 3:00 PMSmall-group pasta and gelato cooking class with Towns of Italy (Via Panicale). We eat what we make, finishing around 6 PM Booked ahead
  9. 7:00 PMSunset at whichever viewpoint we missed yesterday
  10. 8:30 PMA light dinner: drinks over the Ponte Vecchio at the Hotel La Scaletta rooftop bar if it's open, or walk-in pizza at Mister Pizza or LiVio Pizzeria

Tip: Once you leave any museum in the Pitti complex you can't re-enter. Some Oltrarno shops may be closed on Easter Sunday.

Day 4 Mon, Apr 6 · Pisa & Lucca

The Leaning Tower of Pisa & cycling Lucca

An early regional train to Pisa for the Piazza dei Miracoli and a climb up the Leaning Tower, then on to Lucca for an afternoon of cycling the Renaissance walls with food and wine tastings. We're back in Florence for a late cheese-wheel pasta dinner.

  1. 7:00 AMCoffee and a croissant to go from the hotel. Small bags only today
  2. 7:28 AMRegional train from Florence SMN to Pisa Centrale, arriving 8:42 Booked ahead
  3. 8:45 AMWalk to the Piazza dei Miracoli, home to the Cathedral, Baptistery, Leaning Tower and Camposanto
  4. 9:10 AMA quick visit to the Baptistery of St. John (included with the Pisa ticket) Booked ahead
  5. 9:45 AMClimb the Leaning Tower on a timed slot (arrive by 9:30; bags may need to go in the cloakroom), plus the cathedral's marble floors and bronze doors and the frescoed Camposanto on the same ticket Booked ahead
  6. 12:00 PMCoffee and pastry at Pasticceria Salza near the square, then back to Pisa Centrale
  7. ~12:30 PMShort regional train from Pisa to Lucca (tickets at the station or in the app)
  8. 1:00 PMA light lunch near the station: In Pasta – Cibo e Convivio, Fiaschetto or Ciacco on Piazza Napoleone
  9. 2:20 PMLucca Bikes & Bites small-group tour (Viator) from the Tourist Center Lucca bike rental. We cycle the tree-lined Renaissance walls and the medieval streets with food and wine tastings along the way, until about 5:15 Booked ahead
  10. ~5:30 PMTrain from Lucca back to Florence, arriving around 6:30
  11. 9:00 PMDinner at Osteria Pastella, 5 minutes from the hotel, where the chef finishes our pasta in a cheese wheel at the table Booked ahead

Tip: Keep lunch in Lucca light, because the bike tour includes tastings. Osteria Pastella charges €20 if you cancel less than 2 hours ahead.

Day 5 Tue, Apr 7 · Rome

Rome in a day: Colosseum, Pantheon & the Vatican

A full day trip to Rome on the high-speed train. We tour the Colosseum, Forum and Palatine Hill in the morning, see the Pantheon after lunch and the Vatican Museums in the late afternoon, and finish with the Spanish Steps and Trevi Fountain before the late train home.

  1. 6:30 AMLeave the hotel for Firenze SMN
  2. 7:05 AMFrecciarossa 9591 from Florence to Roma Termini, a 90-minute ride arriving 8:35 Booked ahead
  3. 8:35 AMCappuccino and cornetto at Pasticceria Sessa 1930 by Termini, then the walk to the Colosseum
  4. 9:45 AMGuided tour of the Colosseum, Roman Forum and Palatine Hill with The Tour Guide (be there by 9:30; it runs until about 1 PM) Booked ahead
  5. 1:00 PMLunch: sit down at Pasqualino Al Colosseo dal 1956 (reserved online), or grab a quick sandwich at Mizio's Street Food, Sto Bene Roma or All'Antico Vinaio Booked ahead
  6. After lunchWalk Via dei Fori Imperiali toward Piazza Venezia, past the Forum ruins
  7. 2:00 PMThe Pantheon, one of Rome's most impressive ancient interiors Booked ahead
  8. 3:15 PMTaxi to the Vatican, booked in the Free Now app
  9. 4:00 PMVatican Museums guided tour: check-in at 4, tour at 4:30, covering the Sistine Chapel, the Raphael Rooms and the vast collection. Afterward we walk Via dei Coronari or take a taxi to the Spanish Steps Booked ahead
  10. 7:00 PMThe Spanish Steps and Piazza di Spagna in the evening light
  11. 7:30 PMTrevi Fountain with ground-level access tickets, then gelato at Gelato in Trevi Booked ahead
  12. 8:15 PMWalk or taxi to Roma Termini, arriving by 8:45
  13. 9:10 PMFrecciarossa 9568 back to Florence, arriving 10:58 PM. We sleep on the train Booked ahead

Tip: Eat a real lunch. We're inside the Vatican for hours after 4 PM, and there's little time for dinner before the train.

Day 6 Wed, Apr 8 · Venice

Florence to Venice & a first evening on the canals

A slow morning, then the high-speed train to Venice. We spend the afternoon wandering with no plan and end the night in St. Mark's Square, lit up after dark.

  1. 9:00 AMA slow breakfast at the hotel
  2. 10:45 AMCheck out of Hotel L'Orologio and walk 10 minutes to Firenze SMN
  3. 11:20 AMFrecciarossa 9414 from Florence to Venezia Santa Lucia, arriving 1:34 PM Booked ahead
  4. 1:45 PMWalk or take a water taxi from the station and check in at Palazzo Veneziano Booked ahead
  5. 3:00 PMOur first stroll: get pleasantly lost in the calli, stop for cicchetti and an early aperitivo at a bacaro, then walk to the Rialto Bridge and along the Grand Canal
  6. 7:00 PMDinner at Impronta Restaurant Venice, 15 minutes from the hotel Booked ahead
  7. 8:30 PMAn evening walk through Piazza San Marco, lit at night, before tomorrow's crowds
Day 7 Thu, Apr 9 · Venice

St. Mark's, the Doge's Palace & the islands

A morning inside St. Mark's Basilica and the Doge's Palace, then the vaporetto out to Murano for glass-blowing and Burano for its colorful houses and lace.

  1. 8:00 AMBreakfast at the hotel, leaving by 8:50 for the 20-minute walk to the tour
  2. 9:30 AMWalks of Italy "Legendary Venice" tour of St. Mark's Basilica (including the terraces) and the full Doge's Palace, 9:30–12:30. We meet at the Correr Museum, Piazza San Marco 52, under the portico; look for the green Walks sign Booked ahead
  3. 1:00 PMLunch nearby or on the islands, then the vaporetto to Murano and Burano
  4. 8:00 PMDinner at San Silvestro Restaurant Booked ahead

Tip: Take vaporetto Line 4.1 or 4.2 from Fondamente Nove to Murano, then continue to Burano. Budget 3–4 hours for both islands, including at least 90 minutes on Burano.

Day 8 Fri, Apr 10 · Prosecco Hills

A full day in the Prosecco Hills

A full-day vineyard tour in the UNESCO-listed Conegliano Valdobbiadene region, with rolling hills, historic estates and tastings of Prosecco where it was born.

  1. 8:15 AMWalk to Venezia Santa Lucia. The tour company provides the train tickets to the meeting point (Viator) Booked ahead
  2. All dayProsecco Hills UNESCO tour through the Conegliano Valdobbiadene wine region Booked ahead
  3. 5:00 PMTrain back to Venice (tickets provided by the tour), arriving around 6 PM Booked ahead
  4. 7:30 PMDinner at a restaurant near the hotel

Tip: Reserve dinner before the day starts. You'll be tired and hungry when you get back and won't want to search.

Day 9 Sat, Apr 11 · Florence

Venice to Florence & a final night

We take the FlixBus from the mainland back to Florence for one last night at the Grand Hotel Cavour and a final dinner of our own choosing.

  1. 9:00 AMBreakfast at the hotel
  2. 11:00 AMCheck out of Palazzo Veneziano and head to Venice Mestre: vaporetto or walk to Piazzale Roma, then a local train or bus across the causeway. We pick up drinks and snacks for the ride
  3. 12:10 PMFlixBus Route 466 from Venice Mestre (Stazione FS) to Florence Villa Costanza, arriving 3:40 PM
  4. 3:40 PMTaxi or tram from Villa Costanza and check in at the Grand Hotel Cavour for one night
  5. EveningFinal dinner in Florence. It's our last free evening, and most of the center is walkable from the hotel

Tip: Venice Mestre is on the mainland, not in the historic center. Confirm your route there the night before.

Day 10 Sun, Apr 12 · Florence

Arrivederci, Italia

A very early start for a 7 AM flight home from Florence. Ten days done.

  1. 4:30 AMVery early ride to Florence Airport
  2. 7:00 AMFly home from Florence, checking our bags at the counter

Tip: Pack the night before, leave everything by the door and set multiple alarms. Check your bags, since they'll likely be overweight after ten days of shopping and souvenirs.

Our tips

  • Keep tickets on your phone

    Our train tickets were in Google Wallet and our tour tickets in Apple Wallet. Check each ticket for its check-in instructions, review them before every departure, and screenshot everything before you leave home. Bring passports, since you'll need them for all travel.

  • Respect the timed entries

    Some slots are strict and some are flexible, so know which is which. Be in line at Giotto's Campanile by 2:05 PM for a 2:15 slot, check in at the Vatican at 4 PM sharp, show your Palazzo Pitti ticket before 1 PM for priority access, and arrive at the cooking class by 2:45 PM for a 3 PM start.

  • Pack layers and good shoes

    April in Italy is mild but variable. Comfortable shoes are non-negotiable on the cobblestones, and you'll want a light jacket for Venice evenings.

  • Travel light on climb and bike days

    No big bags are allowed on the Campanile climb, and the Lucca bike tour calls for a small day bag. Leave everything else at the hotel.

  • Catch the sunset

    In early April the sun sets around 7:45 PM in Florence. Be at the viewpoint by 7. With two evenings, try both Piazzale Michelangelo and San Miniato al Monte. San Miniato is quieter and better for photos on a clear night.

  • Taxis and money

    Download the Free Now app for taxis in Rome. Uber works in some Italian cities. Cards are accepted almost everywhere, but small cash is handy for cafés and markets.

  • Eat like a local

    Aperitivo hour runs roughly 6–8 PM, so don't miss it. In Venice, cicchetti are snacks at a bacaro (wine bar). Coffee means espresso, and it's cheaper standing at the bar. Many restaurant kitchens don't open until 7:30 PM or later, and gelato is the perfect follow-up to a sandwich lunch.

  • Mind the fine print

    Check cancellation policies (Osteria Pastella charges €20 if you cancel within 2 hours) and check hotel breakfast hours before a late night.

  • Learn a few words of Italian

    Locals appreciate the effort. Buongiorno (good morning), buonasera (good evening), per favore (please), grazie (thank you), un tavolo per due (a table for two), il conto, per favore (the check, please) and cin cin! (cheers) go a long way.
