# Notes for Claude

- After committing and pushing work on a branch, open a pull request into `main` without asking first.
  Tony & Sharon asked for this so finished trips can go live by merging.
- Adding a trip: follow "Adding a trip" in `README.md` and the format in `content/SCHEMA.md`, then run
  `python3 tools/build.py` and commit the generated files in `site/` too.
- Keep private details (texts, emails, phone numbers, addresses, booking codes, where tickets are stored)
  out of the published pages.
- Family is fine to include: Tony & Sharon are happy for their sons, Josh and Ryan, to be named in the
  itineraries and to appear in photos. Other people (friends, strangers) stay out unless they say so.
