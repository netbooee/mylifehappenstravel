By Tony & Sharon · Traveled June 2023

This was a big loop through Northern California: four nights on Fisherman's Wharf in San Francisco, the redwoods and the Marin coast, two nights of wine tasting in Calistoga, the wild Mendocino coast, and a few days on Lake Tahoe before heading home on the Fourth of July.

The San Francisco days were packed on foot, from Lombard Street to Chinatown, with an early dinner before the evening ferry to Alcatraz. Once we picked up the car the pace slowed down: one or two big plans a day, lots of coastline, and a good dinner at the end of it. We still ran out of time for a few things, so we've left those in as ideas for next time.

The plan, day by day

Day 1 Fri, Jun 23 · San Francisco

Newark to San Francisco

An afternoon flight across the country, a rental car at SFO, and our first night at the Argonaut Hotel on Fisherman's Wharf.

  1. 12:30 PMLyft to Newark airport Booked ahead
  2. 3:29 PMFly Alaska Airlines 293 Newark → San Francisco, landing at SFO at 6:53 PM Booked ahead
  3. 7:30 PMPick up the rental car at the Hertz counter Booked ahead
  4. EveningCheck in at the Argonaut Hotel on Fisherman's Wharf Booked ahead
  5. A late, easy dinner near the hotel
Day 2 Sat, Jun 24 · San Francisco

Lombard Street to Chinatown, then Alcatraz at night

A full day on foot: the crooked street, chocolate, the maritime park, the sea lions on Pier 39, the Ferry Building farmers market and Chinatown. An early dinner on the wharf set us up for the evening ferry to Alcatraz.

  1. 8:30 AMWalk from the hotel to a coffee shop for breakfast
  2. MorningWalk Lombard Street, the "crookedest street in the world"
  3. 9:00 AMGhirardelli Chocolate Experience at Ghirardelli Square
  4. 10:00 AMSan Francisco Maritime National Historical Park (included with the National Park Pass)
  5. Late morningThe sea lions and the shops on Pier 39
  6. MiddayGraze our way through the Ferry Building farmers market (Saturdays 8 AM – 2 PM) to hold us until an early dinner
  7. AfternoonGolden Gate Fortune Cookie Factory, then a walk around Chinatown
  8. Walk to Union Square for a little shopping
  9. 4:00 PMEarly dinner at Scoma's on Fisherman's Wharf, a 10-minute walk from the Alcatraz ferry at Pier 33
  10. 5:55 PMAlcatraz Night Tour ferry from Pier 33 (be there by 5:30) Booked ahead
  11. LateIce cream at Ben & Jerry's on the way back to the hotel

Tip: Book the Alcatraz night tour well ahead: it sells out weeks in advance, and they want you at Pier 33 25 minutes before your ferry.

Day 3 Sun, Jun 25 · Marin & San Francisco

Muir Woods, Point Bonita & biking the Golden Gate

An early start for the redwoods at Muir Woods and the Point Bonita Lighthouse, a rest at the hotel, then rental bikes out to the Golden Gate Bridge and the Palace of Fine Arts.

  1. 7:30 AMLeave the hotel in the rental car and pick up Starbucks on the way
  2. 8:30 AMMuir Woods National Monument: our parking reservation was for 8:30 (bring the printed parking pass and the National Park Pass) Booked ahead
  3. 1:00 PMA quick lunch at In-N-Out Burger
  4. AfternoonPoint Bonita Lighthouse in the Marin Headlands
  5. Back to the hotel to rest
  6. 4:00 PMPick up bikes from Blazing Saddles at Pier 41 and ride out to the Golden Gate Bridge Booked ahead
  7. Ride back past the Palace of Fine Arts
  8. EveningDinner outside at The Buena Vista, home of the famous Irish coffee

Tip: Muir Woods parking is by reservation only, and the early slots are much quieter. Download a hiking app (and the trail map) before you go: cell service in the canyon is spotty.

Day 4 Mon, Jun 26 · Point Reyes & Calistoga

Point Reyes, then into wine country

We checked out of the Argonaut and took the long way to Calistoga: a morning at Point Reyes National Seashore, an afternoon tasting, and dinner on the main street in town.

  1. 9:00 AMCheck out of the Argonaut, grab something to eat and drive north
  2. 10:00 AMExplore Point Reyes National Seashore and have lunch there
  3. Plan B if Point Reyes is fogged in: Sonoma Plaza for shops and lunch, or Mission San Francisco de Solano (about an hour from Point Reyes)
  4. 3:00 PMTasting at Madrigal Family Winery (about 1.5 hours from Point Reyes) Booked ahead
  5. Check in at the Inn on Pine in Calistoga, five minutes from the winery Booked ahead
  6. Walk the Calistoga shops (Copperfield's Books is open until 7 PM)
  7. 6:30 PMDinner outside at Calistoga Inn & Brewery, walkable from the inn Booked ahead
Day 5 Tue, Jun 27 · Calistoga

Old Faithful Geyser & two wineries

Calistoga's own geyser in the morning, then an afternoon of tastings without anyone having to drive.

  1. 9:00 AMOld Faithful Geyser of California after breakfast (it opens at 8:30) Booked ahead
  2. 1:00 PMTasting at Bennett Lane Winery, five minutes away on the free Calistoga Shuttle Booked ahead
  3. 3:30 PMTasting at Tank Garage Winery, a short walk from the inn Booked ahead
  4. 6:30 PMDinner at Evangeline, walkable from the inn Booked ahead

Tip: The Calistoga Shuttle is free and picks you up door to door around town. Call ahead to book a pickup so tasting days don't need a designated driver.

Day 6 Wed, Jun 28 · Mendocino

The winding road to Mendocino

We checked out of the Inn on Pine and took the long, windy drive through Anderson Valley to the coast and the Brewery Gulch Inn.

  1. MorningCheck out of the Inn on Pine and drive toward the coast
  2. LunchLunch at Mosswood Market in Boonville
  3. AfternoonCoffee in Mendocino village
  4. Check in at the Brewery Gulch Inn Booked ahead
  5. A walk at Big River Beach
  6. Stock up at the grocery store in town
  7. EveningDinner and the evening wine hour at the Brewery Gulch Inn

Tip: Route 128 through Anderson Valley is curvy for the last stretch to the coast. Anyone who gets carsick should take something before Boonville.

Day 7 Thu, Jun 29 · Fort Bragg & Mendocino

Skunk Train railbikes, Glass Beach & the headlands

A day up and down the coast: pedal-powered railbikes through the redwoods out of Fort Bragg, Glass Beach and Laguna Point, then the Mendocino Headlands with the drone.

  1. MorningBreakfast at the Brewery Gulch Inn
  2. 10:00 AMDrive about 20 minutes to Fort Bragg and check in for the Skunk Train railbike tour
  3. 10:30 AMSkunk Train Railbike Tour along the old logging line Booked ahead
  4. The model train museum
  5. AfternoonCoffee and a walk along Fort Bragg's Main Street
  6. Glass Beach, with sea glass smoothed by decades of waves
  7. Laguna Point in MacKerricher State Park, a short boardwalk to the harbor seals
  8. Mendocino Headlands State Park, where Tony flew the drone over the bluffs
  9. EveningBack to the Brewery Gulch Inn for dinner

Tip: The railbikes are electric-assisted, so there's no real pedaling effort. Check in on time: the tour leaves right on schedule.

Day 8 Fri, Jun 30 · Mendocino to San Francisco

Russian Gulch & the coast road south

One last coastal hike, then the long drive back down Highway 1 to San Francisco for a night in the city.

  1. MorningBreakfast at the Brewery Gulch Inn
  2. Russian Gulch State Park: the Headlands Trail to the sea cave and the Devil's Punch Bowl
  3. Check out and drive south on Highway 1
  4. AfternoonStops along the way: Salt Point State Park, Jenner, or Bodega Bay if the drive runs long
  5. Something to eat on the road
  6. EveningDinner in San Francisco: Boa or ramen
Day 9 Sat, Jul 1 · Lake Tahoe

Over the mountains to Lake Tahoe

We drove east from San Francisco to South Lake Tahoe and checked in at Marriott's Timber Lodge, right in Heavenly Village.

  1. Drive about 3.5 hours from San Francisco to South Lake Tahoe
  2. Check in at Marriott's Timber Lodge in Heavenly Village Booked ahead
Day 10 Sun, Jul 2 · Lake Tahoe

A free day at the lake

No plans on purpose: time by the water and around Heavenly Village.

Day 11 Mon, Jul 3 · Lake Tahoe

Canoeing at Sand Harbor

An early start to beat the crowds at Sand Harbor on the Nevada shore, with its clear water and granite boulders, and a canoe out on the lake.

  1. 6:15 AMLeave the hotel and drive about 35 minutes to Sand Harbor (the lot opens at 7 AM)
  2. 8:00 AMClearly Tahoe three-person clear-bottom canoe (get there by 7:30) Booked ahead
  3. 6:45 PMItalian dinner at Izabella, walkable from the hotel Booked ahead

Tip: Sand Harbor's parking lot fills up very early on summer holiday weekends. Arrive when it opens or you may be turned away.

Day 12 Tue, Jul 4 · Lake Tahoe to San Francisco

Back to San Francisco and home

We checked out of the Timber Lodge and drove back to San Francisco on the Fourth of July.

  1. Check out of Marriott's Timber Lodge
  2. Drive back to San Francisco

Our tips

  • Bring the National Park Pass

    It covered the San Francisco Maritime National Historical Park and Muir Woods entry. Muir Woods still needs a separate parking reservation, printed or on your phone.

  • Eat early on Alcatraz night

    The night tour ferry leaves around 6 PM. A 4 PM dinner at Scoma's, a short walk from Pier 33, meant we weren't starving on the island.

  • Don't overpack the city days

    We planned more than we could do in San Francisco. Cable cars, the Painted Ladies, Golden Gate Park, Haight-Ashbury and the Musée Mécanique arcade all had to wait for next time. A Muni day pass or Clipper card makes the cable cars easy if you do get to them.

  • Let the shuttle drive in Calistoga

    The free Calistoga Shuttle does door-to-door pickups around town, which makes a two-winery afternoon easy. Call ahead to book it.
