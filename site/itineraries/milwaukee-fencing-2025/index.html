By Tony & Sharon · Traveled June 2025

Josh's Summer Nationals meant a week built around two fencing days at the Baird Center, with everything else fit around them: a landing day downtown, a full day trip to Chicago, and a couple of easier mornings before his later bout on the last day. The Trade put us a short walk from the convention center, the RiverWalk and the Third Ward, so we barely needed the car-free city's transit beyond The Hop streetcar.

We kept fencing days simple – coffee and snacks instead of a sit-down breakfast, home base close enough to shower and regroup between rounds – and used the lighter days for the RiverWalk, Lakefront Brewery, brunch at the Pfister and a full day across the border in Chicago on the Hiawatha.

The plan, day by day

Day 1 Mon, Jun 30 · Milwaukee

Landing day – the Baird Center, the RiverWalk & the Third Ward

A morning flight in, a quick stop at the fencing venue to cheer on a friend and get Josh's gear checked, then an afternoon easing into downtown Milwaukee on foot before our first dinner reservation.

  1. 9:25 AMFly United flight UA3554 from Newark (EWR), landing in Milwaukee (MKE) at 10:57 AM Booked ahead
  2. 11:00 AMUber to The Trade, Autograph Collection to check in or at least hold our luggage Booked ahead
  3. 12:00 PMWalk to the Baird Center to catch Lottie fencing 70+ Vet Women's Épée, if we make it in time, and have Josh do his equipment check
  4. Lunch – walk and see what's on the way; Craft at the hotel is the fallback if we're hungry and nothing else stands out, though it's pricier with fewer casual options
  5. Back to the hotel to drop off fencing gear, freshen up and get properly checked in
  6. AfternoonWalk the Milwaukee RiverWalk into the Historic Third Ward – a scenic 3-mile river path with public art and bridges, then cobblestone streets, galleries and boutiques just a few minutes further on foot
  7. Lakeshore State Park for the pebbly shoreline, prairie landscaping and the fishing pier where it links up with the Oak Leaf and Hank Aaron trails
  8. 6:30 PMDinner at The Social American Tavern, 611 N Broadway (OpenTable reservation) Booked ahead

Tip: Quick water and snack stops downtown: Tourist Convenience at 240 W Wells St, right between the hotel blocks; The Grand Convenient about 0.3 miles away; or the Walgreens at 275 W Wisconsin Ave, about a 5-minute walk.

Day 2 Tue, Jul 1 · Chicago

A day trip to Chicago on the Hiawatha

The Amtrak Hiawatha makes Chicago an easy day trip from Milwaukee: Millennium Park and The Bean, a walk along the Riverwalk, deep dish or lobster rolls for lunch, and an afternoon at Navy Pier before the train back for a lighter dinner at the hotel.

  1. 7:15 AMLeave the hotel and walk to the Milwaukee Intermodal Station
  2. 8:00 AMDepart Milwaukee on the Amtrak Hiawatha Booked ahead
  3. 9:30 AMArrive at Union Station, Chicago
  4. 10:00 AMMillennium Park & The Bean – Cloud Gate, Crown Fountain and Lurie Garden, with skyline views and photos
  5. 10:45 AMWalk the Chicago Riverwalk east toward the lake – bridges, architecture and people-watching, with a cold drink stop at Tiny Tapp or Island Party Hut
  6. 12:00 PMLunch – Giordano's deep dish at 730 N Rush St (call ahead around 11:30 AM to have it ready by noon) or Luke's Lobster at 134 N LaSalle St for a faster lobster or crab roll
  7. 1:30 PMWalk or Uber to Navy Pier, 600 E Grand Ave, for lake views, shops and a lemonade or ice cream
  8. 2:00 PMRide the Centennial Wheel – about 15 minutes with skyline and lake views Booked ahead
  9. 3:15 PMCoffee or a donut break – Do-Rite Donuts or the Offshore Café on the pier, if we want one
  10. 4:00 PMWalk or Uber back to Union Station, aiming to be there by 4:45 PM
  11. Return train to Milwaukee on the Hiawatha Booked ahead
  12. 6:45 PMWalk back toward the hotel, keeping an eye out for a better dinner option along the way
  13. 7:15 PMDinner reservation at Craft, the hotel restaurant (OpenTable) – nothing too heavy, since Josh fences tomorrow Booked ahead

Tip: Other quick lunch options near the Riverwalk: Brown Bag Seafood Co. at 340 E Randolph St for seafood bowls and tacos, America's Dog & Burger inside Navy Pier for classic Chicago dogs, Small Cheval near River North for quick-serve burgers, or Big City Chicken inside Navy Pier for fried chicken sandwiches. Bring comfortable shoes, sun protection, a refillable water bottle and a light layer for the lake breeze.

Day 3 Wed, Jul 2 · Milwaukee

Josh's Division 2 Men's Épée, Lakefront Brewery & Lake Park

Josh's first fencing day at the Baird Center, then an afternoon at Lakefront Brewery for the tour and the cheese curds, a walk through Lake Park toward Juneau Park, and ice cream before an early night.

  1. 6:45 AMCoffee and a bite from the hotel lobby, or bars and snacks brought along for breakfast
  2. 7:30 AMLeave the hotel and walk to the Baird Center, 400 W Wisconsin Ave
  3. 8:00 AMJosh's Division 2 Men's Épée tournament at the Baird Center Booked ahead
  4. Back to the hotel after fencing for Josh to shower
  5. 1:30 PMWalk to Lakefront Brewery
  6. 2:00 PMLakefront Brewery Tour, 1872 N Commerce St – Uber instead if we're short on time Booked ahead
  7. After the tour, grab a table for lunch if one's free – the cheese curds are supposed to be excellent
  8. 5:30 PMWalk to Lake Park, explore, and walk south toward Juneau Park
  9. 7:00 PMPurple Door Ice Cream, 205 S 2nd St (open until 10 PM) – good reviews and unique flavors
  10. 8:00 PMHead back toward the hotel and get ready for tomorrow

Tip: Lakefront Brewery's tours run on a schedule and the on-site restaurant fills up fast right after – grab a table as soon as the tour ends if lunch there is the plan.

Day 4 Thu, Jul 3 · Milwaukee

A slow morning, brunch at the Pfister & Division 1A

A late start, brunch at the Pfister before the lunch menu takes over, Josh's second fencing day in the afternoon, and a low-key dinner around the corner from the hotel once we're packed for tomorrow's early flight.

  1. 9:00 AMSleep in
  2. 10:00 AMWalk to brunch at Cafe at the Pfister, 424 E Wisconsin Ave – breakfast is only served until 11 AM, when the lunch menu takes over; no reservation, so we can detour if another spot catches our eye
  3. 1:00 PMLeave the hotel and walk to the Baird Center
  4. 2:00 PMJosh's Division 1A Men's Épée tournament at the Baird Center Booked ahead
  5. Back to the hotel after fencing for Josh to shower, and start packing – a very early departure tomorrow
  6. 7:30 PMDinner at Oak Barrel Public House, around the corner from the hotel (reviews are mixed, but it's convenient on a packing night)

Tip: Café at the Pfister stops serving breakfast at 11 AM sharp – go early if the morning menu is the point.

Day 5 Fri, Jul 4 · Milwaukee → Newark

Early flight home

A 6 AM flight means leaving the hotel in the dark, but we're back in Newark by mid-morning.

  1. 3:45 AMLeave the hotel for the airport
  2. 6:05 AMFly United Airlines from Milwaukee (MKE) Booked ahead
  3. 9:25 AMLand back in Newark (EWR)

Our tips

  • The Hop streetcar is free

    No tickets or vouchers needed – just hop on and off at any stop along the downtown route. It runs every 10–20 minutes (Mon–Fri 5 AM–12 AM, shorter hours on weekends) and connects Milwaukee Intermodal, the Third Ward, East Town, the Lower East Side and downtown – handy for stretches between the RiverWalk, Third Ward or lakeshore areas that we don't feel like walking.

  • Pace fencing days around the schedule, not the clock

    Grab-and-go coffee and snacks instead of a sit-down breakfast, and keep the hotel close enough to shower and regroup between rounds. The Baird Center is a short walk from The Trade, which made both tournament days easier.

  • If there's extra time

    The Pabst Mansion and the Milwaukee Art Museum are both worth a visit if the schedule allows, and the Milwaukee Public Market in the Third Ward is a good casual lunch stop.
