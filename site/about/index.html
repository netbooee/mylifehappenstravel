About us

Hi, we're Tony & Sharon.

We travel together, and we plan together: flights, hotels, tours, and above all the dinner reservations. My Life Happens Travel is where we keep those plans and share them.

How we travel

Our trips have a rhythm: active mornings with the big sights and the tours we've booked, looser afternoons to wander, and evenings at a table we've been looking forward to all day. We book the things that sell out well ahead, and leave room for everything else.

Why we write it all down

Every trip starts as a detailed plan: times, tickets, addresses and reservations. After we get home, we tidy that plan up and post it here, so anyone planning the same trip can borrow as much or as little of it as they like.

Where we've been

So far on the site: Canada, England & France, Ireland, Italy, Spain, USA, with more trips on the way. Counting every flight, not just these trips, we've flown 144,772 miles. The numbers are below.
