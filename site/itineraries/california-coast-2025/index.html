By Tony & Sharon · Traveled July 2025

This trip traced the California coast in all its variety: the pastoral calm of Carmel Valley Ranch, the dramatic cliffs of Big Sur and Cambria, the maritime wonder of Monterey, the rolling fog of Half Moon Bay, the wine country of Calistoga and Napa, the wild headlands of Mendocino, and finally San Francisco to close the loop.

Eighteen days was enough to slow down and actually feel each place rather than just drive through it. Almost every stop got at least two nights, and the pacing reflects that. A rental car was essential: the coast is meant to be driven on your own schedule.

The plan, day by day

Day 1 Thu, Jul 24 · Carmel Valley

Arrival day: SFO to Carmel Valley Ranch

We flew into San Francisco, picked up our rental SUV and drove about two hours south to Carmel Valley Ranch, a luxury resort tucked into the Santa Lucia mountains with a working organic garden, lavender fields, an infinity hot tub and vineyard views.

  1. 9:00 AMFly Newark → San Francisco, landing at SFO at 12:15 PM Booked ahead
  2. 12:15 PMPick up our intermediate SUV at the SFO rental car center (via AirTrain) Booked ahead
  3. 1:30 PMLunch and road-trip supplies around Millbrae/Burlingame
  4. ~4:00 PMDrive about 2 hours south on US-101 and check in at Carmel Valley Ranch Booked ahead
  5. 7:00 PMDinner at Valley Kitchen, the resort's farm-to-table restaurant using produce from its own garden Booked ahead
  6. After dinnerFirst night in California on the balcony, with soft, warm valley air

Tip: Thursday afternoon traffic leaving the Bay Area can add 30–45 minutes to the two-hour drive, so plan for it.

Day 2 Fri, Jul 25 · Carmel Valley

Ranch day: hike, pool & Folktale Winery

A slow day on the ranch: a morning hike or garden visit, a long afternoon at the pool and infinity hot tub, then dinner in a winery garden with live music.

  1. 8:00 AMFree coffee in the lobby and a pastry from The Market & Creamery on the ranch grounds
  2. MorningChoose an activity: the gentle Corral Hike, the moderate 3-mile Huff 'N' Puff + Horseshoe Trail Loop with vineyard views, or the organic garden and lavender field
  3. AfternoonPool and the infinity hot tub overlooking the valley, one of the highlights of the property
  4. 3:30 PMRanch Roundup at the Lodge Lobby: a fun, complimentary 20-minute visit with the farm animals
  5. 6:00 PMDinner in the wine garden at Folktale Winery & Vineyards, with live music until 7 PM Booked ahead

Tip: The lavender field peaks in July; early morning light gives the best photos before the midday sun flattens the color.

Day 3 Sat, Jul 26 · Carmel-by-the-Sea & Cambria

Carmel-by-the-Sea, then south to Cambria

We left the ranch for a morning in the storybook village of Carmel-by-the-Sea before driving south to Cambria Beach Lodge for two nights.

  1. MorningCoffee at the ranch, check out at 11 AM and leave our luggage with the concierge
  2. 11:30 AMDrive 20–25 minutes via Carmel Valley Road to Ocean Avenue and park near Ocean & Lincoln
  3. NoonLunch at Café Carmel: sandwiches, paninis, quiche, pastries and espresso
  4. AfternoonWalk Ocean Avenue's galleries and boutiques down to Carmel Beach, then the Scenic Bluff Path above the sand
  5. 3:00 PMCollect our bags at the ranch and drive about 2 hours south to Cambria Beach Lodge Booked ahead
  6. EveningSunset on the 1-mile Moonstone Beach boardwalk, looking for sea otters, pelicans and harbor seals
  7. 7:00 PMDinner at Robin's Restaurant, a Cambria institution with a garden setting (Sea Chest Oyster Bar, across from the hotel, is the walk-in alternative) Booked ahead

Tip: Sections of Route 1 between Big Sur and Cambria have been intermittently closed. Check Caltrans before driving south and take Hwy 101 to Hwy 46 West if needed.

Day 4 Sun, Jul 27 · Cambria & San Simeon

Hearst Castle, elephant seals & a winery

A full day north of Cambria: a morning tour of Hearst Castle, the elephant seal rookery at Piedras Blancas, a pier lunch in San Simeon and a tasting at Hearst Ranch Winery.

  1. 8:30 AMContinental breakfast at the hotel, then a 30-minute drive north on Route 1
  2. 9:20 AMHearst Castle Grand Rooms Tour: the Neptune Pool, Casa Grande and the Celestial Suite Booked ahead
  3. 11:00 AMPiedras Blancas Elephant Seal Rookery, 4 miles north, plus a walk on the Boucher Trail toward the lighthouse (free)
  4. 12:30 PMWalk San Simeon Pier, then pick up our lunch from Seaside Foods at Sebastian's General Store Booked ahead
  5. 1:30 PMLegacy Tasting at the Hearst Ranch Winery tasting room in San Simeon (about 90 minutes)
  6. 3:30 PMOptional browse of Cambria's West and East Villages
  7. 7:00 PMDinner at The Black Cat Bistro: seasonal California cuisine Booked ahead

Tip: Hearst Castle wants a printed confirmation letter and photo ID at the Visitor Center ticket window, not just a phone screen. Print before you leave home.

Day 5 Mon, Jul 28 · Monterey

Arrive in Monterey: Cannery Row & the Coastal Trail

A scenic 90-minute drive north on Route 1 brought us to the InterContinental The Clement, right on historic Cannery Row.

  1. MorningBreakfast and check-out at Cambria Beach Lodge, then drive north on Route 1
  2. NoonCheck in at the InterContinental The Clement Monterey and stroll Cannery Row Booked ahead
  3. AfternoonPool time or the Monterey Bay Coastal Trail: southwest toward Lovers Point for seals and tide pools, or northeast to Fisherman's Wharf and the Coast Guard Pier for sea lions
  4. EveningNo dinner plans: Old Fisherman's Grotto and Monterey Fish House are local seafood favorites

Tip: The kelp beds around Cannery Row are prime sea otter habitat. Early morning and late afternoon are the best times to spot them.

Day 6 Tue, Jul 29 · Monterey

Whale watch & a coastal afternoon

Monterey Bay is one of the best whale watching spots in the world: humpbacks, blue whales, orcas, dolphins and sea birds are all possible.

  1. 7:45 AMCoffee from the Starbucks on Cannery Row, a short walk from the hotel
  2. 8:30 AMCheck in for the 9 AM Monterey Bay Whale Watch (about 3–4 hours on the water) Booked ahead
  3. 1:00 PMRelaxed lunch near the wharf on Cannery Row
  4. 2:30 PMRest in the room or by the pool
  5. 4:30 PMWalk the Monterey Bay Coastal Trail: the flat 2-mile stretch to Lovers Point Park in Pacific Grove, or past the aquarium and McAbee Beach to the Coast Guard Pier

Tip: The bay is much colder than the shore. Dress warmer than you think you need to: base layer, fleece, windproof jacket, long pants and closed shoes, plus snacks, binoculars and sunscreen.

Day 7 Wed, Jul 30 · Monterey & Half Moon Bay

Monterey Bay Aquarium & the drive to Half Moon Bay

A morning at one of the finest aquariums in the world, then two hours north along the coast to the fog-draped headlands of Half Moon Bay.

  1. 9:00 AMBreakfast at Tidal Coffee, then check out and leave our bags at the hotel
  2. 10:00 AMMonterey Bay Aquarium: the three-story Kelp Forest, Open Sea, sea otters, jellyfish galleries and the tide pool touch tanks (allow 2.5–3 hours) Booked ahead
  3. 1:00 PMCollect our bags and drive about 2 hours north on Route 1, stopping at any turnout that looks good
  4. 3:00 PMCheck in at the Beach House Hotel, Half Moon Bay, right on the Coastside Trail Booked ahead
  5. EveningDinner within a 15-minute walk: Barbara's Fishtrap (cash only, no reservations) or Half Moon Bay Brewing Company

Tip: Barbara's Fishtrap is cash only with no reservations. Arrive by 5:30–6 PM if you want a table.

Day 8 Thu, Jul 31 · Half Moon Bay

Coastside Trail, Pillar Point Harbor & Sam's

A relaxed day on the San Mateo coast, ending with dinner a two-minute walk from the hotel.

  1. MorningFree continental breakfast at the Beach House Hotel
  2. DaytimeWalk or bike the Coastside Trail (3 miles each way), or rent kayaks from Half Moon Bay Kayak Co. at Pillar Point Harbor; the hotel pool and Main Street are other options
  3. 7:30 PMDinner at Sam's Chowder House: lobster bisque, clam chowder and headland views from the deck Booked ahead

Tip: The Maine Lobster Roll and New England clam chowder are Sam's signature orders, and the outdoor deck tables book fast.

Day 9 Fri, Aug 1 · Half Moon Bay & Bay Area

A slow Half Moon Bay morning, then on to our Vrbo

An easy transition day between the coast and a Vrbo rental for the next two nights.

  1. MorningHotel breakfast and a walk on the Coastside Trail or the beach before checking out
  2. AfternoonExplore, run errands and find a spot for lunch on the way
  3. 4:00 PMCheck in to our Vrbo rental
Day 10 Sat, Aug 2 · Muir Woods

Muir Woods: redwoods at dawn

An early start for one of the most awe-inspiring old-growth redwood groves in Northern California, with coast redwoods over 1,000 years old and 250 feet tall.

  1. 7:45 AMLeave for Muir Woods to make our 8–8:30 AM parking window Booked ahead
  2. MorningThe 2-mile Main Trail loop (about 90 minutes) through the Bohemian and Cathedral Groves, plus a side trail up the hillside
  3. AfternoonFlexible: the Marin Headlands, the Sausalito waterfront and Point Reyes are all nearby

Tip: Muir Woods requires a timed parking reservation; you can't drive in without one. Bring a National Park Pass and layers, as the grove stays cool even in August.

Day 11 Sun, Aug 3 · Calistoga

North to Calistoga

We checked out of the Vrbo and drove north into wine country for two nights at the Inn on Pine in Calistoga.

  1. 10:00 AMCheck out of the Vrbo and load the car
  2. ~6:00 PMArrive at the Inn on Pine, Calistoga, roughly 2 hours from the Bay Area Booked ahead
  3. EveningWalk to Calistoga Creamery for ice cream (open until 9:30 PM)

Tip: If you'll arrive after 6 PM, call the Inn on Pine ahead; the lobby is open until 10 PM.

Day 12 Mon, Aug 4 · Calistoga

Calistoga tastings: Bennett Lane & Tamber Bey

A full day of tastings in Napa Valley's northernmost wine town, with ice cream to close the evening.

  1. MorningContinental breakfast at the Inn on Pine
  2. 11:30 AMTasting at Bennett Lane Winery, known for its Maximus red blend and Cabernet Sauvignon Booked ahead
  3. 1:00 PMLunch in town (Brannan's Grill or Sam's Social Club at Indian Springs), then a wander past the boutiques and spa storefronts
  4. 3:30 PMTasting at Tamber Bey Winery, on a working equestrian estate with horses roaming the grounds Booked ahead
  5. EveningDinner in town and a second visit to Calistoga Creamery

Tip: Calistoga sits on a geothermal field: if time allows, a 60-minute mud bath at Indian Springs or Dr. Wilkinson's is the quintessential Calistoga experience.

Day 13 Tue, Aug 5 · Mendocino

To Mendocino through the Anderson Valley

About 2.5 hours via Hwy 128 brought us to Brewery Gulch Inn on the south edge of Mendocino, one of California's most atmospheric coastal towns.

  1. MorningBreakfast and an 11 AM check-out from the Inn on Pine
  2. MiddayLunch stop in Boonville, halfway there, at the Boonville Hotel Restaurant
  3. ~3:00 PMArrive at Brewery Gulch Inn and explore the headlands and town Booked ahead
  4. 5:30 PMComplimentary wine hour at the inn (5:30–6:30 PM) with local Mendocino coast wines and snacks

Tip: Download offline maps before leaving Calistoga: Hwy 128 and the Mendocino coast have spotty or no cell service.

Day 14 Wed, Aug 6 · Mendocino

Mendocino outdoors: ferns, cliffs & Big River

Our one full day in Mendocino was for getting outside: canyon hikes, cliff-top state parks or a paddle up a redwood-lined estuary.

  1. MorningFull breakfast at Brewery Gulch Inn, one of the best included breakfasts on the coast
  2. MorningPick one: Van Damme State Park's Fern Canyon Trail to the pygmy forest, Mendocino Headlands State Park, the 3-mile round trip to Russian Gulch's 36-foot waterfall, or kayaking Big River with Catch a Canoe & Bicycles Too
  3. AfternoonLunch in Mendocino village and a browse of the galleries and shops
  4. 5:30 PMSecond evening of the inn's complimentary wine hour

Tip: Ask the staff at the wine hour where to eat and which trails to walk; the innkeepers are a genuine resource.

Day 15 Thu, Aug 7 · Napa Valley

Mendocino to Napa: pool, hot tub & dinner outdoors

We said goodbye to the coast and drove southeast into Napa Valley for one night at Alila Napa Valley.

  1. MorningFinal breakfast at Brewery Gulch Inn and an 11 AM check-out; optional last park visit or shopping
  2. MiddayDrive about 2.5 hours via Hwy 128 through the Anderson Valley
  3. ~2:00 PMCheck in at Alila Napa Valley, with welcome wine waiting in the room Booked ahead
  4. AfternoonA proper resort afternoon at the pool and infinity hot tub
  5. 7:30 PMDinner outside at Salva Terrace & Bar, then the welcome wine on our balcony Booked ahead

Tip: Alila's poolside dining runs 11 AM–2 PM, so an early arrival can mean lunch by the pool.

Day 16 Fri, Aug 8 · San Francisco

Into San Francisco and the Palace Hotel

A two-hour drive south took us to the Palace Hotel, one of San Francisco's grand historic hotels, opened in 1875, with the Garden Court at its center.

  1. MorningBreakfast at Alila and an 11 AM check-out
  2. 1:00 PMCheck in at the Palace Hotel, then explore Union Square and SoMa on foot Booked ahead
  3. EveningNo plan: the Ferry Building Marketplace (15-minute walk) for casual bites, or Bix, Zuni Café or Foreign Cinema for a sit-down dinner

Tip: Have at least one meal in the Palace's Garden Court; it's one of the most architecturally spectacular dining rooms in the country.

Days 17–18 Sat, Aug 9 – Sun, Aug 10 · San Francisco

A free day in the city & flying home

One unplanned day to wander San Francisco, then a final morning before the flight home.

  1. SaturdayBreakfast at the hotel, then a full day exploring: the Embarcadero, the Ferry Building, Chinatown, North Beach and the waterfront on foot or by cable car
  2. SundayBreakfast, an 11 AM check-out and last sights; leave for SFO by 1 PM to return the car
  3. 4:20 PMFly San Francisco → Newark, landing at 1:05 AM Booked ahead

Tip: The SFO rental car return is off-site and needs the AirTrain. Budget 30–45 minutes for it and 2.5–3 hours at the airport in total.

Our tips

  • Plan around Route 1 closures

    Sections of Route 1 south of Big Sur have been closed. Check Caltrans before you drive and use inland alternatives such as Carmel Valley Road, US-101 and Hwy 46 West.

  • Pack layers, always

    The coast in summer runs 55–70°F with foggy mornings, while Napa and Calistoga can hit 85–100°F in July and August. Bring a light jacket for every coastal evening, and dress extra warm for the whale watch.

  • Choose a car with room

    We moved hotels every two nights or so, mostly on Route 1 and US-101, so an SUV with good storage space made life easier.

  • Download offline maps for Mendocino

    Cell service along Hwy 128 and the Mendocino coast is patchy or missing in many stretches.

  • Print the Hearst Castle confirmation

    The Visitor Center ticket window asks for a printed confirmation letter and photo ID.

  • Buy two bottles at Hearst Ranch Winery

    The $35 Legacy Tasting fee is waived with a two-bottle purchase, and they'll box the wine for the car.

  • Bring cash for the seafood shacks

    Barbara's Fishtrap and Sea Chest Oyster Bar are cash only with no reservations, so arrive early.

  • Use the hotel perks

    Brewery Gulch Inn has a complimentary wine hour from 5:30 to 6:30 PM daily, the Beach House Hotel and the Palace Hotel included breakfast, and Alila Napa Valley left welcome wine in the room.
